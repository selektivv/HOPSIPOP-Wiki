# Bait

Bait is a one-cast consumable that adds rarity luck to your next catch. Each of the four types has its own texture and can be purchased through `/fish shop` in the **Bait Shop**.

## How to Use Bait

1. Hold a fishing rod in your main hand.
2. Put the bait in your offhand.
3. Cast the line.

One bait is consumed as soon as you cast. Its luck bonus applies to that cast, and an action-bar message confirms which bait was used.

## How Rarity Luck Works

Bait improves the rarity of a MasterFish after the game has successfully rolled one. It does not increase the 65% base chance that a vanilla catch becomes a MasterFish; use a stronger [Fishing Rod](rods.md) or a Treasure Crystal for that.

After the initial rarity roll, every successful luck check moves the catch up one rarity tier. The chance is halved after each successful upgrade, so even strong bait does not turn every catch into a Legendary or Mythic fish. Bait luck combines with rod luck and an installed Luck Crystal.
