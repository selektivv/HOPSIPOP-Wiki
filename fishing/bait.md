# Bait

Bait is a one-cast consumable that adds rarity luck to your next catch. There are 100 different baits across ten tiers, each with its own resource-pack texture. Bait is no longer sold in `/fish shop`: recipes are researched and crafted at the [Fishing Table](fishing-table.md).

<img src="../assets/fishing-table/bait-textures.png" width="800" alt="All 100 Fishing Table bait textures, arranged from tier 1 to tier 10">

The preview contains one row per tier. Within every row, the ten designs represent Bait, Lure, Chum, Jig, Morsel, Wader, Tempter, Bloom, Grub, and Charm.

## How to Use Bait

1. Hold a fishing rod in your main hand.
2. Put the bait in your offhand.
3. Cast the line.

One bait is consumed as soon as you cast. Its luck bonus applies to that cast, and an action-bar message confirms which bait was used.

## Bait Tiers

Every tier contains ten baits. Their exact luck increases by 0.2 percentage points from the first design in the row to the last.

| Tier | Family | Rarity-luck range | Ingredient tier | Recipe amounts |

| 1 | Meadow | +3.0% to +4.8% | 1 | 2 + 1 |
| 2 | Whisper | +7.5% to +9.3% | 1 | 2 + 2 |
| 3 | Ember | +12.0% to +13.8% | 2 | 3 + 2 |
| 4 | Glacier | +16.5% to +18.3% | 2 | 3 + 2 + 1 |
| 5 | Verdant | +21.0% to +22.8% | 3 | 3 + 2 + 2 |
| 6 | Storm | +25.5% to +27.3% | 3 | 4 + 2 + 2 |

The amount pattern shows how many of each ingredient a recipe uses. The actual ingredient combination differs between the ten baits in a family and is shown in the Fishing Table's research catalog.

## Unlocking and Crafting Bait

Each recipe requires you to have caught its listed fish species at least once and to carry the displayed ingredients. Researching consumes those ingredients, permanently unlocks the recipe for your account, and gives you the first bait. Afterward, you can reproduce it in the Fishing Table's shapeless 3×3 crafting grid.

See [Fishing Table](fishing-table.md) for the table recipe, all ingredient sources and chances, and the complete research and crafting process.

## How Rarity Luck Works

Bait improves the rarity of a MasterFish after the game has successfully rolled one. It does not increase the 65% base chance that a vanilla catch becomes a MasterFish; use a stronger [Fishing Rod](rods.md) or a Treasure Crystal for that.

After the initial rarity roll, every successful luck check moves the catch up one rarity tier. The chance is halved after each successful upgrade, so even the strongest Void bait does not turn every catch into a Legendary or Mythic fish. Bait luck combines with rod luck and an installed Luck Crystal.
