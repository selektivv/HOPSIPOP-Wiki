# Fishing Rods

MasterFishing rods improve the chance that a vanilla catch becomes a MasterFish, add rarity luck, and provide slots for [Augment Crystals](augment-crystals.md). Buy them through `/fish shop` and select **Rod Shop**.

## Rods and Stats

The listed fish chance applies each rod's multiplier to the 65% base MasterFish chance. [Treasure Crystals](augment-crystals.md#treasure) add percentage points afterward, and the final chance cannot exceed 100%.

## What the Stats Mean

- **Fish chance** determines whether a vanilla catch is replaced with a MasterFish. If the roll fails, the original vanilla catch remains.
- **Rarity luck** can upgrade a successful MasterFish catch after its initial rarity is rolled. It does not increase the chance of receiving a MasterFish.
- **Augment slots** determine how many different crystal types the rod can hold. Each type occupies one slot, regardless of its tier.

A vanilla fishing rod can still catch MasterFish with the 65% base chance, but it has no custom rod luck or augment slots.

## Choosing a Rod

Start with the free Apprentice Rod. Higher tiers become especially useful when you want to combine several augments: for example, Treasure for a higher MasterFish chance, Luck for rarities, and Titan for larger fish. The Grandmaster Rod already reaches the 100% MasterFish cap before Treasure is added.
